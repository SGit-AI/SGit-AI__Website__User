The state of play on this site, kept here rather than in a chat message. Numbered so a reply can refer to an item without quoting it. As of 21 Oct 2026.

Asks back to the project lead

#AskWhy it needs youStatus
N1The name. The billing article left it open: user.sgit.ai and api.user.sgit.ai, or billing.sgit.ai and api.billing.sgit.ai. Answered on 21 October 2026: user.sgit.ai, the first of the two, mapped to this repository with GitHub Pages. v0.1.0 was built as users.sgit.ai and never deployed under that name; v0.1.1 renamed it. Whether the billing part later stands on its own at billing.sgit.ai, so others can use it, was not part of the answer and stays an option.The CNAME, the relying party of every passkey made here, and the network directory entry all follow from it, and a passkey made for one host does not answer another.Answered
N2Who creates the first vault, and with whose access key? The account model has the user paste an access key once; the billing article asks whether the service should make a user's first vault for them when they first log in.It decides whether a new user needs anything from us before they have an account, which is the whole point of the design.Open
N3Is this site the key hub? Two ways across sites are named: a hub that holds the master and hands each site the key to its own vault after a pop-up, or Related Origin Requests. If user.sgit.ai is the hub, it is the strictest origin in the network and every sibling connects to it; if not, each site keeps its own relying party and the hub is secrets.sgit.ai.A design choice with a security boundary in it. Both are consistent with the articles; neither is decided.Open
N4Should the upload server accept unvalidated credit? Where usage costs real money, it may want only validated credit, or a small amount on top.A commercial call, from the article's own open questions.Open
N5How anonymous is the usage that flows into the admin vault? Enough to bill, and no more; but what is enough?A privacy boundary only the owner can set.Open
N6Which workflows should this site run itself, beyond the lab? Today it describes, and the newsroom does. Making a vault here needs the vendored library (T1) and an access key in memory; the relying party is now settled as user.sgit.ai.Scope: a reference site, or the account's home.Open

Published unresolved

Not tasks. Questions the site carries in the open rather than answering for tidiness:

  • Which side of the QR path owns the PRF mismatch. The lab can say each path was deterministic and the two disagreed; it cannot say which is right. The design does not need to know, and the report to Apple and Google is the newsroom's step 12.
  • Whether a cold unlock of two to four seconds of key stretching is acceptable on a phone, or whether the keys should stay in session storage for the tab, as the beta does.
  • Whether the consolidated view should show a page read on two devices once or twice. The meter's rule today is once per session per device; the newsroom's experiment 2.
  • How often the browser should sync for billing, once the per-sync cost is known from the newsroom's experiment 1.

user.sgit.ai

The account is a vault, and the user holds it: a passkey opens a door, the door opens a master, the master holds the keys, and the user's browser keeps the record and the meter. No sign-in to the site, no account database, no server of ours that can read a thing. CC BY 4.0, part of the sgit.ai network.
